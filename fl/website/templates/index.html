About Me

NOMAD Project Manager,
Belgian Institute for Space Aeronomy,
Avenue Circulaire 3,
Uccle 1180,
Belgium

I am a member of the Planetary Atmospheres group at the Belgian Institute for Space Aeronomy (BIRA-IASB). We study the atmospheres of planets, to understand the composition and the atmospheric processes occurring in the other bodies of our Solar System, in particular Venus and Mars.

My current project is NOMAD, a spectrometer orbiting around Mars on the ESA ExoMars Trace Gas Orbiter satellite. NOMAD was primarily designed and built in Belgium, as part of a large multinational team of engineers and scientists, and is operated from BIRA-IASB via the European Space Agency (ESA).

Since NOMAD launched in 2016, I am responsible for planning observations, calibrating the data, day-to-day management activities such as organising team meetings and telecons, and production of publicly available products for the ESA Planetary Science Archive. I also created and maintain the NOMAD website on behalf of the team. During the Manufacturing, Assembly, Integration and Testing (MAIT) phase of the mission, I was responsible for management of the project at BIRA-IASB, and led the ground calibration campaign prior to launch.

My previous work, in the Department of Atmospheric, Oceanic and Planetary Physics at the University of Oxford, centred on supporting laboratory measurements for the Diviner Lunar Radiometer on the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter, a satellite orbiting the Moon.

NOMAD

NOMAD HDF5 file downloader

A tool for selecting and downloading NOMAD HDF5 data, written in Python/Django (note: downloads require a valid NOMAD team email address)

NOMAD file tree browser

A tool for tracing files as they pass through the NOMAD data pipeline. A list of missing files can be downloaded for further investigation

ExoMars TGO tracker

Displays the real-time location of TGO orbiting Mars. Written in Python/Flask with a call to the ESA WebGeoCalc API and plotted with Vega-Lite

NOMAD observation planning tables

Lists all nadir and solar occultation observations made by NOMAD on ExoMars TGO. Data is extracted from an SQLite3 database generated by the observation planning software

NOMAD SO channel diffraction orders

Monitoring tool, showing how often each diffraction order has been measured by the NOMAD SO channel

NOMAD COP table viewer

A clickable viewer for checking all available SO and LNO observations that can be run with the current onboard COP tables
