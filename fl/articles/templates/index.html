The tables are now very large. To constrain by observation start date, you can specify start and end dates or MTP numbers in the address bar, separated by an ampersand (&), as follows:

utc_start=YYYY-MM-DD

utc_end=YYYY-MM-DD

mtp_start=integer (inclusive)

mtp_end=integer (inclusive)


For example:

To display all occultations in January 2020: irthomas.eu/fl/nomad_planning/occultations?utc_start=2020-01-01&utc_end=2020-02-01

To display all nadirs in MTPs 9 and 10: irthomas.eu/fl/nomad_planning/occultations?mtp_start=9&mtp_end=10
